# Codex Cosmic privacy notice

Effective September 12, 2026. Codex Cosmic is published by Haki, the community publisher operating the Sxholar/codex-cosmic repository. Support and privacy contact: [sxholarr@gmail.com](mailto:sxholarr@gmail.com). This notice describes the local application.

## What the application stores

Cosmic stores projects on your computer. Project information can include names, design prompts and briefs, text and control values, pages and layers, images you import or generate, reference URLs, comments, and design-guidance files you attach. Depending on what you enter, this content may include personal information.

Cosmic also stores local run statuses, timestamps, summaries and proposed results so you can inspect work, recover after refreshes and handle conflicting edits. The activity log retains up to 80 recent events per project. Other project and run files do not currently expire automatically.

The browser stores prompt drafts and saved design-system preferences in local storage. The local service records project selection, but new studio launches show project home and do not automatically reopen a saved design. Legacy browser selection keys may remain unused. Cosmic does not add advertising trackers or analytics collection to the studio.

## Why information is used

Content is used to display, edit and save your design; produce requested model-generated changes; handle image preparation; preview interactions; export files; and report run progress or errors.

## Services that may receive information

When you request a Codex design run, Cosmic sends the design request, relevant saved design content and up to four selected image references to your local Codex CLI. The CLI communicates with OpenAI under your own account. Enabled design-guidance content is included in the design context. The publisher does not receive your account credentials through Cosmic.

Host image generation, image editing and reference browsing use the services and permissions of your Codex conversation. Their providers may receive the prompts, images or URLs needed to carry out your request. Their own policies and your account settings govern that processing.

First launch may download locked software dependencies from npm. Downloading a release or opening support pages uses GitHub. Those services receive ordinary connection information under their own policies. These downloads do not upload your Cosmic projects.

The local release has no publisher-operated project-storage service and no automatic project-upload or telemetry feature. If you choose to export or share a file, the destination you choose receives that file. Exported project JSON includes the project's saved content and image assets.

## Storage and controls

Data lives in the per-user Cosmic application folder, a folder you configure, or the data folder of an original standalone checkout. It remains until removed by the user. Removing an installed plugin does not necessarily remove its saved application data or browser storage.

You can export your projects, edit content, pause or remove attached design guidance, stop active runs, and move projects to recoverable Trash. Trash is not permanent deletion. Deleting an item from a design may leave copies in prior exports, original image files or saved run results. For complete local removal, close the service and remove its application data, relevant exports/backups, and browser site storage. OpenAI or other providers' retained copies are governed by their own controls.

## Support and privacy requests

Public bug reports can be filed at https://github.com/Sxholar/codex-cosmic/issues. For private support or privacy requests, email [sxholarr@gmail.com](mailto:sxholarr@gmail.com). Do not post private projects, passwords or account tokens in public issues.

If you contact support, Haki receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. Email is processed through Gmail to respond to your request and remains in the publisher's mailbox until deleted. Public issues remain on GitHub according to GitHub's controls. You may request deletion of information supplied directly to the publisher using the privacy email above. Please send only the information needed to explain your request.

The publisher can address information submitted directly through support, but cannot access or delete files held only on your computer. This notice must be revised before introducing hosted accounts, server-side project storage, telemetry or additional recipients.
